# Crow Notch Island Motion Storyboard Brief

This brief is for generating a motion storyboard image that shows Crow Island fused with the Mac notch.

## Image Generation Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: macOS notch-fused island motion storyboard for Crow

Primary request:

Create a high-fidelity motion storyboard for Crow Island, a Mac notch-fused AI assistant control surface. The design must show the island physically merging with the MacBook notch and expanding downward from it, not floating below it. Show a sequence of 5 frames from left to right or top to bottom: fused idle, listening, running expansion, approval expansion, collapse to completed.

Scene/backdrop:

A realistic MacBook top-screen crop with a visible black notch and macOS menu bar. The Crow interface in the background should be softly blurred and secondary. The focus is the notch and the island's shape transformation.

Subject:

Five storyboard frames showing the same top-center notch:

1. Fused idle: the black notch extends downward into a small black capsule with crow mark and "Crow". No visible gap between notch and island.
2. Listening: the fused black capsule widens slightly, a teal waveform appears inside, text "Listening...".
3. Running expansion: the black notch stretches downward through a centered neck into a larger rounded panel, text "Research AI desktop assistants", "Extracting sources...", teal progress bar.
4. Approval expansion: the panel grows wider and warmer, still physically connected to the notch by a black/amber neck, text "Approval Required", "Level 2 - Reversible", "Approve", "Reject", "Open Task".
5. Collapse complete: the panel contracts back upward into a compact green-black fused capsule, text "Research report ready", "Open".

Style/medium:

High-fidelity product interaction storyboard, native macOS UI design, precise and premium. Show subtle ghosted transition silhouettes or arrows only if they help explain the motion. The island should feel like a living extension of the Mac notch.

Composition/framing:

16:9 landscape storyboard. Use clean spacing between frames. Each frame must include the notch and the connected island shape. The approval expanded frame should be the largest and most detailed.

Lighting/mood:

Bright neutral macOS environment, soft realistic shadows, calm and focused.

Color palette:

Black notch and graphite island body, muted teal active accents, amber approval accents, green completion accent, off-white translucent panel surface. Avoid purple gradients.

Materials/textures:

Native macOS translucent material, black notch material, soft border, restrained shadow. The connector between notch and panel must be visible and physically integrated.

Text:

Use these exact visible labels where possible:

- "Crow"
- "Listening..."
- "Research AI desktop assistants"
- "Extracting sources..."
- "Approval Required"
- "Level 2 - Reversible"
- "Approve"
- "Reject"
- "Open Task"
- "Research report ready"
- "Open"

Constraints:

- The island must be fused with the notch in every frame.
- There must be no visual gap between notch and island in idle/listening.
- Expanded states must grow downward from the notch through a connector neck.
- Do not draw unrelated floating cards.
- Do not make it a full dashboard.
- Do not use chat bubbles.
- Do not use humanoid assistants or mascots.
- No copyrighted product logos.
- No watermark.
- Keep labels legible.

Avoid:

- Detached notification banners.
- Cards randomly floating under the menu bar.
- Purple SaaS gradient styling.
- Dark cyberpunk UI.
- Tiny unreadable text.
- Overly diagrammatic arrows that dominate the image.
